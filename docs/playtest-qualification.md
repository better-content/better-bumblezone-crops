## Primary implemented gameplay promise

The mod's fixed `src/main/resources/defaults/cultivars.json` catalog defines imported plants, their propagules, produce, maturity rules, placement adapters and Bumblezone origin dimension. `CultivarChunkFinalizer` places up to 24 living pollen nurseries on pollen in **new** Bumblezone chunks only; `LivingPollenNurseryBlock#getDrops` supplies four catalog seeds with source metadata. `CultivarLootModifier` filters unsolicited/off-origin propagules and limits off-origin harvest reimbursement to a mature, sourced planting (persistent-harvest crops excluded), while mature origin crops can propagate. `CultivarPlantings` tracks provenance. `WildCropForage` makes seven Farmer's Delight wild vegetable blocks food-only, not a renewable seed source; the flower policy constrains relocation versus multiplication. `RootminEcologyHandler`/`FirethornEcologyPolicy` provide bounded Bumblezone ecology. These are present source behaviors, **not** a claim of end-to-end pack verification or that every catalog entry is obtainable in every generated world.

Acquisition/identity references for a sampling or recipe-root checklist: `defaults/cultivars.json` (`seedItem`, `plantBlocks`, `produce`, `originDimensions`), `BumblezoneCultivars.DEDICATED_SEEDS`, `CultivarCatalog`, `LivingPollenNurseryBlock`, `CultivarChunkFinalizer`, `CultivarLootModifier`, `WildCropForage.PRODUCE`, `CultivarAcquisition` (provider-owned aliases and multipart anchors), `data/better_bumblezone_crops/tags/items/seeds.json`, `data/better_bumblezone_crops/tags/{items,blocks}/origin/bumblezone.json`, and `data/forge/loot_modifiers/global_loot_modifiers.json`. For example, `minecraft:carrot` produce is distinct from `better_bumblezone_crops:minecraft_carrot_seeds`; the seven wild plants yield their named food rather than seed stock. The catalog is an identity/eligibility map, **not** a recipe list or proof of natural acquisition; inspect actual installed datapacks, loot, trade and native providers for each recipe root. No local recipe/GUI suite establishes pack-wide cooking or screen accessibility.

## Remaining acceptance gaps / bounded authoring decision

The repository-local deterministic and native tests cover important boundaries but **do not** qualify full-pack recipe-root resolution, GUI usability, installed-pair provenance, or the complete gameplay/recipe-root/GUI/scale suites required before the friends' world. Pack sampling should use one world per candidate (default seed **424242**) and examine actual nursery/seed and produce acquisition alongside pack recipes and screen behavior. New-chunk-only finalization and persisted block-entity seed IDs require real save/reopen and upgrade checks; do not regenerate or reset a shared world as a substitute. This repo has no native real-client reconnect or 8/16/32-player at-distance-10 performance/concurrency gate: a serial fake-player probe cannot prove 32 simultaneous real logins, chunk throughput, client rendering or UI behavior. Record persistence, multiplayer and measured performance separately from unit, native GameTest and full-pack results.

No new local regression was authored here: the bounded cultivar/wild/flower/finalization checks already have source and/or native coverage, and fabricating another source-level assertion would not close the missing real-client, world-reopen, recipe/GUI or multiplicity gate. The exact **unrun native gaps** for this authoring pass are canonical `verifyFull` (11 required GameTests plus data/provider proofs) and isolated `runNurseryGameTestServer` (one finalization test); neither was executed or authorized. Full-pack and real-client/persistence/load gates also remain unrun and must be evaluated by their respective owners after authoring review and explicit human approval for each applicable test run.
